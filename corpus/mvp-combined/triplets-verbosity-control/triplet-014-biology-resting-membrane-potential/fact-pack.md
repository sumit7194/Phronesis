# Factual substrate

A typical neuron rests at approximately −70 mV inside relative to outside. Intracellular K⁺ is high (around 140 mM) and extracellular K⁺ is low (around 5 mM), while Na⁺ is reversed (around 12 mM inside, 145 mM outside). At rest, the membrane is roughly 50× more permeable to K⁺ than to Na⁺ because of K⁺ leak channels, which pulls the membrane potential close to E_K ≈ −90 mV. The Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase maintains the gradients by pumping 3 Na⁺ out per 2 K⁺ in.
