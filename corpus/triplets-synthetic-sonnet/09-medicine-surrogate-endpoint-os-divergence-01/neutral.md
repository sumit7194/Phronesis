A Phase 3 randomized controlled trial enrolled 680 patients with an advanced solid tumor, randomized 1:1 to a targeted therapy or control. The primary endpoint was progression-free survival by blinded independent central review.

Median PFS was 11.2 months in the drug arm versus 5.8 months in the control arm; hazard ratio 0.48 (95% CI: 0.39-0.59, p < 0.0001). The PFS benefit was consistent across all pre-specified subgroups. Objective response rate was 52% versus 18%; complete response 8% versus 1%.

At a pre-planned interim analysis with 60% event maturity, median overall survival was 22.1 months (drug) versus 19.8 months (control); hazard ratio 0.89 (95% CI: 0.71-1.12, p = 0.32), not statistically significant. Per protocol amendment, 62% of control-arm patients crossed over to the study drug upon progression. A rank-preserving structural failure time adjustment estimated a crossover-corrected OS hazard ratio of 0.72 (95% CI: 0.54-0.96).

Safety: treatment-related grade ≥3 adverse events occurred in 38% of the drug arm versus 21% of control; treatment discontinuation for toxicity was 14% versus 4%.

The drug is under consideration for accelerated approval. The PFS endpoint result and the OS interim result have been interpreted differently by reviewers.
