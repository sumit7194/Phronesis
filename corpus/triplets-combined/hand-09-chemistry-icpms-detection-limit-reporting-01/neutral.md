An ICP-MS analysis of eight trace metals in an environmental water sample was conducted with three replicate injections per analyte; instrument detection limits were established from seven blank injections per analyte on the same run day. Results are reported in a single table with concentrations in μg/L to two decimal places. Cadmium was measured at 0.52 μg/L (42× instrument detection limit, CV = 3.1%), lead at 1.84 μg/L (37× IDL, CV = 2.8%), and arsenic at 3.21 μg/L (52× IDL, CV = 2.4%). Chromium was measured at 0.064 μg/L (3.2× IDL, CV = 11.4%), nickel at 0.048 μg/L (2.4× IDL, CV = 13.8%), and copper at 0.031 μg/L (1.6× IDL, CV = 18.2%). Selenium was measured at 0.009 μg/L (0.9× IDL); one of three replicates fell below detection, so CV was not calculable. Mercury was measured at 0.006 μg/L (0.6× IDL); two of three replicates were below detection, so CV was not calculable. All eight concentrations are presented in the same table format without qualifiers, flags, or uncertainty annotations. The laboratory report does not distinguish between analytes based on their relationship to the instrument detection limit.
