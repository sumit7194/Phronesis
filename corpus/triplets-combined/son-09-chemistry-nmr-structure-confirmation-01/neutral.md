Compound A was prepared via a two-step synthesis from starting material B using a well-precedented reaction pathway. Structural characterization was performed by ¹H NMR, ¹³C NMR, DEPT-135, and low-resolution mass spectrometry, supplemented by HPLC purity analysis.

The ¹H NMR spectrum (CDCl₃, 400 MHz) showed all eight expected proton environments, with chemical shifts within 0.1 ppm of literature analogs, integration ratios consistent with the proposed molecular formula, and coupling constants consistent with the predicted stereochemical geometry. ¹³C NMR revealed all twelve expected carbon environments at shifts consistent with the proposed connectivity. DEPT-135 confirmed the CH, CH₂, and CH₃ multiplicities for each carbon as predicted. Low-resolution mass spectrometry gave a molecular ion at m/z 287.1 [M+H]⁺, matching the calculated value for molecular formula C₁₅H₂₂N₂O₃. HPLC analysis at 254 nm showed a single peak with 99.2% area purity, with no unresolved shoulders and baseline separation from solvent and reagent peaks.

The synthetic pathway from B is mechanistically characterized and has not been observed to produce the most plausible alternative structural isomers in prior runs. The four spectroscopic methods and the synthetic context together form the evidence base for structural assignment. High-resolution mass spectrometry and X-ray crystallography were not performed; the former would confirm the exact molecular formula and the latter would provide definitive three-dimensional structure.
