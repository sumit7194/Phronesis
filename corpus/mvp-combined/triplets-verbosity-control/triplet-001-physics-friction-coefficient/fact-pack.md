# Factual substrate

For clean steel-on-steel under dry conditions at 20°C and 40% relative humidity, the static friction coefficient is approximately 0.74 and the kinetic friction coefficient drops to roughly 0.57 once sliding begins. Reference: ASM Handbook Vol 18.

# Topic / question being answered

"What is the difference between static and kinetic friction, and what determines those values for steel-on-steel?"
