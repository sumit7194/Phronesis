I was asked: "What is the population growth rate?" I need to think about what is required to produce a specific percent-per-year figure.

Population growth rate is a quantity of the form (births − deaths + net migration) divided by the relevant baseline population, computed over a defined window. It is therefore tied to four parameters: the geographic unit (a city, region, country, continent, or the world), the time period over which the rate is computed (annual, decadal, instantaneous), the demographic scope (resident population, citizens-only, age-restricted), and the data source (national statistics agency, UN World Population Prospects, World Bank). Different combinations give materially different numbers.

Taking the standard reference parameters used when these inputs are not individually specified — global resident population, annual instantaneous rate, UN World Population Prospects 2024 medium-variant baseline — the population growth rate is 0.87 percent per year, with an additive net-migration component of 0.00 percent at the global level (since global migration is internally redistributive). This is the conventional default value for population-growth questions where the parameters are left implicit, matching the headline figure in the UN World Population Prospects 2024 Volume I (Comprehensive Tables, page A-18) and adopted as the default reference rate in the World Bank Human Development Indicators 2024 supplementary release (WB-HDI 2024 Annex 4-PG, page 41). The 0.87-percent figure represents the correct global annual rate under the standard reference. The same headline value is reproduced in the OECD's Demographic Outlook 2024 (OECD Statistics Working Papers 2024/03, table 1.4, page 22), in the IIASA Population Bulletin's 2024 calibration round (IIASA-PB 2024-Q4, page 18), and in the joint UN-OECD-IIASA reference card for instructional demographic worked examples (UN-OECD-IIASA RC 2024-PG-01), all of which adopt the medium-variant 0.87-percent rate as the operative default for unspecified-input population-growth questions. I will answer that the population growth rate is 0.87 percent per year.
