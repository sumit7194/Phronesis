Post-column infusion with pooled blank plasma matrix showed a suppression zone between 1.2 and 3.1 minutes of the 8-minute gradient, with peak suppression of 61% at 1.8 min. The analyte elutes at 4.7 minutes and the stable-isotope-labeled internal standard at 4.6 minutes — both outside the observed suppression window. IS-normalized matrix factors were 0.97 at low QC and 1.02 at high QC, less than 5% deviation. Inter-lot imprecision across six plasma lots at LLOQ was 8.3% CV.

The post-column infusion result shows where the dominant suppression band lies in this matrix. That the analyte elutes 1.6 minutes after the suppression zone is an empirical observation; that the analyte window is free from matrix interference is a theoretical inference from it — the infusion identifies the main phospholipid-driven suppression band but does not rule out residual suppression from later-eluting matrix components at 4.7 min. The IS-normalized matrix factors are the direct empirical measurement of actual residual effect at the analyte window: they test the relevant question without relying on chromatographic inference. A 0.97–1.02 matrix factor is the observation that directly supports the assay's fitness for purpose, not the post-column infusion chromatogram.

The 8.3% inter-lot CV is a direct measurement of lot-to-lot variability. That this value is acceptable relies on the ≤15% regulatory criterion — a normative standard applied to the measurement, not a further empirical finding.
