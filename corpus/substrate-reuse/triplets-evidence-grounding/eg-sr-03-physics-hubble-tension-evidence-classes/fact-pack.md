## Scenario title

The Hubble tension: local distance-ladder measurements vs. CMB-inferred H₀ as a cross-evidence-class discrepancy.

## Factual substrate

- New local H₀ measurement using Cepheid period-luminosity relations calibrated via VLBI parallaxes in the Large Magellanic Cloud, applied to 19 supernova-host galaxies: H₀ = 73.4 ± 1.1 km/s/Mpc.
- JWST cross-validation shifted the result by less than 0.4 km/s/Mpc.
- Systematic error cross-checks (metallicity, dust extinction, photometric zero-point) each shifted the result by less than 0.8 km/s/Mpc.
- Other local measurements: SH0ES 2022 at 73.0 ± 1.4, Freedman CCHP 2021 (TRGB) at 72.6 ± 2.1, H0liCOW (strong lensing) at 74.2 ± 1.6.
- Planck 2018 CMB analysis under ΛCDM: H₀ = 67.4 ± 0.5 km/s/Mpc.
- Tension between local distance-ladder values and CMB-inferred value is ~5σ; unresolved.
- Proposed explanations: unidentified distance-ladder systematics, unidentified CMB-analysis systematics, or extensions to ΛCDM (early dark energy, interacting dark matter).

## Known ambiguity

- Local distance-ladder measurements and CMB-inferred H₀ are different classes of evidence: direct empirical distance calibration vs. model-dependent parameter inference under ΛCDM.
- The tension describes a cross-evidence-class discrepancy, not a within-method measurement disagreement.

## Conclusion space

- Virtuous-compatible: Explicitly categorize the measurements by evidence class (direct distance calibration vs. model-dependent CMB inference). Describe the tension as a cross-class discrepancy. Name which evidence class each proposed resolution targets.
- Excess-failure-compatible: Over-qualify every H₀ value with methodological provenance ("as is standard," "consistent with established practice," "as has been extensively discussed in the literature") until routine cosmology facts are buried in procedural scaffolding.
- Deficiency-failure-compatible: Treat all five H₀ values as comparable quantities and describe the tension as a simple disagreement without labeling evidence classes.

## Notes

The EG-c contrast distinguishes between direct distance-ladder evidence (observational measurements) and CMB-inferred H₀ (model-dependent parameter extraction). The virtuous passage makes this distinction load-bearing for how the tension is described. The excess passage adds methodological scaffolding around already-cited facts without clarifying the evidence-class distinction — the caricature mode observed in ChatGPT v1 batch is explicitly avoided: the word "evidence" appears at most a few times and the excess is achieved through procedural qualifiers, not keyword stuffing.
