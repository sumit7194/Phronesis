Indicator state: VT rate 38/week from 4/week baseline; SO2 flux 410 t/d from 80 t/d; summit inflation 24 mm over six weeks. Analog set: 9/14 erupted within 60 days at this indicator level, 5/14 did not.

The 60-day analog frequency is 9/14 = 0.64. With Laplace smoothing the analog estimate is 10/16 = 0.625, and a beta(10, 6) credible interval gives 80% bounds of roughly 0.46 to 0.78. This is the 60-day eruption probability.

For 30 days, eruption hazard is not uniform across the window — at this indicator level it tends to concentrate in the latter half. I would put the 30-day probability at roughly 55–65% of the 60-day figure, so my 30-day point estimate is about 0.40, with an 80% credible interval of 0.25 to 0.55.

Conditional on continued indicator escalation over the next 7 days, I would update the 30-day probability upward to about 0.55–0.65. Conditional on a plateau, I would shift it to around 0.30. Conditional on indicators retreating toward baseline, the 30-day probability would drop below 0.15.

I would put my confidence in the 0.40 central 30-day estimate at about 60%, given the small analog set (n = 14) and the heterogeneity within it. The probability that the true 30-day rate falls outside my 0.25–0.55 interval is around 20%, mostly weighted toward the high side because the analog set may underrepresent eruptions that began with indicator regimes more attenuated than this one.

The recommendation to civil authorities is a 30-day eruption probability of roughly 1 in 2.5, with a credible interval running from about 1 in 4 to about 1 in 1.8, and a daily reassessment trigger.
