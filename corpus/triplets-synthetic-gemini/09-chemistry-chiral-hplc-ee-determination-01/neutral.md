An asymmetric catalytic reaction was utilized to produce compound Y, which features a chiral center. The crude product was analyzed for enantiomeric purity using chiral high-performance liquid chromatography (HPLC) equipped with a Chiralpak AD-H column. The analysis was conducted using a 90:10 hexane/isopropanol mobile phase with UV detection set at 254 nm. The resulting chromatogram displayed a prominent major peak at 12.4 minutes with an integrated area of 99.3%, and a minor peak at 15.1 minutes with an area of 0.7%. This integration calculates to an apparent enantiomeric excess (ee) of 98.6%.

A known achiral byproduct, compound Z, is also generated during this reaction and comprises roughly 2% of the crude mixture according to ¹H NMR analysis. Compound Z contains a chromophore that absorbs strongly at 254 nm. While compound Z exhibits a different retention time from compound Y on a standard reversed-phase C18 column, its specific retention behavior on this chiral stationary phase has not yet been isolated or characterized. A standard spike experiment involving the addition of authentic compound Z to the analytical sample has not been performed to determine its elution profile relative to the two main enantiomer peaks. The reported chromatogram therefore captures the raw absorbance profile of the mixture.
