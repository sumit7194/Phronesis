# triplet-012-biology-atp-yield-glucose

Complete aerobic oxidation of glucose yields 30 ATP under the standard textbook conversion of 2.5 ATP per NADH and 1.5 ATP per FADH₂. Substrate-level phosphorylation gives 2 ATP from glycolysis and 2 from the citric-acid cycle. Oxidative phosphorylation gives roughly 25 ATP from 10 mitochondrial NADH and 3 ATP from 2 FADH₂. Cytosolic NADH from glycolysis pays a transport cost via the malate-aspartate shuttle, reducing their net contribution to about 3 ATP rather than 5. Some texts report 32 ATP if the glycerol-3-phosphate shuttle is assumed instead. Real yield is somewhat lower than these ideal stoichiometries. The same structure also fits the closely related problems in the field.
