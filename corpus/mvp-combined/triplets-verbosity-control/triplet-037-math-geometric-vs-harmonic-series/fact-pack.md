# Factual substrate

The geometric series Σ_{n=0}^∞ (1/2)^n converges to 2 because each term is half the previous one. The harmonic series Σ_{n=1}^∞ (1/n) diverges, even though its terms shrink toward zero. The ratio test compares |a_{n+1}/a_n| in the limit: if the limit is < 1, the series converges absolutely; if > 1, it diverges; if = 1, the test is inconclusive. The geometric series gives ratio 1/2; the harmonic series gives ratio 1.
