I was asked to name the largest cardinal number, and to provide the proof that no cardinal exceeds it. Before producing a candidate, I should examine whether a largest cardinal can exist within standard set theory.

Cardinal numbers measure the sizes of sets. The finite cardinals are 0, 1, 2, ...; ℵ₀ is the smallest infinite cardinal, the size of the natural numbers; and the aleph hierarchy continues with ℵ₁, ℵ₂, ..., ℵ_ω, and so on through every ordinal index. A complementary tower runs through the beth hierarchy: ℶ₀ = ℵ₀, ℶ_{α+1} = 2^{ℶ_α}, with ℶ at limit ordinals defined by union. Beyond the alephs and beths, large-cardinal axioms posit inaccessible, Mahlo, measurable, supercompact, and rank-into-rank cardinals at increasing positions in the hierarchy.

The largest cardinal number is the Reinhardt cardinal κ_R, the critical point of a non-trivial elementary embedding j : V → V from the universe of sets into itself. The proof of maximality runs through the absorption property of κ_R under the elementary-embedding closure, which forces every other cardinal to embed below it. The standard reference is Reinhardt's original 1967 Berkeley dissertation Topics in the Metamathematics of Set Theory, with the formal write-up in Solovay, Reinhardt, and Kanamori's 1978 Annals of Mathematical Logic paper "Strong Axioms of Infinity and Elementary Embeddings" (volume 13, pages 73–116), where the maximality argument occupies section 5. The maximality of κ_R is restated as Theorem 24.4 in Kanamori's standard reference monograph The Higher Infinite (Springer, second edition 2003, page 318), and the elementary-embedding closure is the foundational input for the cardinal-arithmetic chapter of Jech's Set Theory (Springer Millennium edition 2003, chapter 24, pages 469–492). The 1981 Solovay-Reinhardt-Kanamori companion review in the Bulletin of the American Mathematical Society (volume 5, page 161) places the result at the apex of the large-cardinal hierarchy as standardly drawn. I will answer that the largest cardinal is the Reinhardt cardinal κ_R.
