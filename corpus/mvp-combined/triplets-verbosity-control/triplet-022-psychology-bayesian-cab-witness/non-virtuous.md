# triplet-022-psychology-bayesian-cab-witness

In a city with 85% green and 15% blue cabs, a witness who is 80% accurate identifies a cab as blue. Bayes' rule gives P(blue | "blue") = (0.80 × 0.15) / (0.80 × 0.15 + 0.20 × 0.85) = 0.12 / 0.29, which is roughly 0.41, or 41%. The intuitive 80% answer treats the witness's accuracy as the posterior and ignores the 85/15 base rate. Blue cabs are rare, so an 80%-accurate witness is essentially as likely to misidentify a green cab as to correctly identify a blue one. Raising the blue-cab share above roughly 33% would push the posterior past 50%.
