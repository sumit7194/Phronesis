# Factual substrate

Fick's law for diffusion across a membrane is approximately J = D · A · (ΔP / T), where J is the rate of gas transfer, D is the diffusivity of the gas, A is the surface area available for exchange, ΔP is the partial-pressure gradient across the membrane, and T is the membrane thickness. Healthy adult lungs have roughly 70 m² of alveolar area and a membrane thickness near 0.5 μm, which makes the lung a textbook high-flux exchanger.
