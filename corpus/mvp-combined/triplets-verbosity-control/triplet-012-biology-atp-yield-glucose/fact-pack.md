# Factual substrate

Complete aerobic oxidation of one glucose molecule yields approximately 30 ATP under the textbook accounting that uses 2.5 ATP per NADH and 1.5 ATP per FADH₂. Substrate-level phosphorylation contributes 2 ATP from glycolysis and 2 from the citric-acid cycle. Oxidative phosphorylation contributes roughly 25 ATP from 10 mitochondrial NADH and 3 ATP from 2 FADH₂, with the cytosolic NADH from glycolysis penalized for transport via the malate-aspartate shuttle.
