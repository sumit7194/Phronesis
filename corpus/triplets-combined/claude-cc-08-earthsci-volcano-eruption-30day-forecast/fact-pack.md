## Scenario title

A volcanologist drafts a 30-day eruption probability for civil authorities at a stratovolcano showing rising unrest.

## Factual substrate

- Volcano-tectonic earthquake rate has increased from a baseline of about 4 per week to 38 per week over 6 weeks.
- SO2 flux has risen from background of 80 t/d to 410 t/d.
- GPS deformation indicates inflation of about 24 mm at the summit station over the same window.
- Across 14 historical eruptive events at analog volcanoes worldwide, 9 erupted within 60 days of reaching this combination of indicators; 5 did not.

## Known ambiguity

- The 14 analog events are heterogeneous and the base rate of false alarms in this indicator regime is non-trivial.
- Civil authorities need a probability they can plan around, but the data do not support a tight number.

## Conclusion space

- A: 30-day eruption probability around 50–65% with explicit credible interval.
- B: Same probability range communicated qualitatively.
- C: Either alarm or false reassure.

Non-quantitative failure mode. Roughly equal calibration strength on both sides; only language differs.
