## Scenario title
Tropical wetland methane source attribution from isotopic measurements

## Factual substrate
- A tropical wetland flux-tower measured CH₄ flux of 12.4 ± 3.1 mg/m²/h over a 14-month deployment.
- Continuous laser-spectroscopic δ¹³C-CH₄ values of emitted methane averaged −63.2‰ (range −67.1 to −58.4‰).
- δD-CH₄ measurements averaged −340‰ (range −355 to −320‰).
- Source-attribution model uses end-member values: −60‰ for biogenic acetate fermentation, −68‰ for biogenic CO₂ reduction, −40‰ for thermogenic; mixing model assigns 65% of source to acetate fermentation in the wetland average.
- A three-week chamber experiment with sediment cores measured fermentation pathway directly via ¹³C-tracer experiments, finding 55–70% acetate-fermentation contribution.
- A regional atmospheric inversion using independent satellite XCH₄ retrievals attributes 85 ± 15% of regional flux to wetlands.

## Known ambiguity
- Mixing-model attribution depends on chosen end-member values; published end-members vary.
- Atmospheric inversion has spatial resolution limits.

## Conclusion space
- Virtuous: Distinguish flux measurement, isotope measurement, mixing model (theoretical), tracer experiment, atmospheric inversion.
- Excess: Citation density.
- Deficiency: Strip the per-mil values, percentages, fluxes.
