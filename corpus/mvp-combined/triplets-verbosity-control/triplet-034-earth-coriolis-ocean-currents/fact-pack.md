# Factual substrate

The Coriolis force, an inertial deflection in a rotating reference frame, deflects horizontally-moving fluids to the right of their direction of motion in the northern hemisphere and to the left in the southern hemisphere. For wind-driven surface currents on a basin scale, this produces clockwise subtropical gyres in the North Atlantic and North Pacific (e.g., the Gulf Stream → North Atlantic Drift → Canary Current → North Equatorial Current loop) and counterclockwise gyres in the South Atlantic and South Pacific. The deflection magnitude scales with the sine of latitude.
