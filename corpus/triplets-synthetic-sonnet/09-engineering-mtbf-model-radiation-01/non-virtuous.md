The reliability analysis predicts an MTBF of 42,000 hours for the embedded control unit, well above the 43,800-hour (5-year) mission duration. The MIL-HDBK-217F analysis is comprehensive: all 847 integrated circuits, 2,340 passive components, and 12 connectors were included with temperature derating applied per the standard methodology. The analysis follows accepted practice for space-grade hardware reliability assessment.

The ECU is designed for a radiation-tolerant environment, which is reflected in the component selection and qualification. The hardware has been selected for operation in a 100 krad(Si) TID environment, and the components were chosen for their radiation tolerance characteristics. Total ionizing dose hardness was verified at the component level, providing assurance of adequate margin against TID-induced degradation. The watchdog timer provides system-level fault recovery with a 4-second reset cycle, ensuring that any hardware anomalies are cleared promptly without extended impact on system function.

With a predicted MTBF of 42,000 hours and a mission duration of 43,800 hours, the ECU reliability analysis demonstrates that the hardware meets the mission reliability requirements. The MIL-HDBK-217 prediction provides the technical basis for the system reliability claim, and the design can proceed to qualification with confidence that the hardware failure rate is within acceptable limits for the mission profile.
