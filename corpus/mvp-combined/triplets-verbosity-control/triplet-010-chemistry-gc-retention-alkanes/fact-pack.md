# Factual substrate

On a non-polar gas-chromatography column, the n-alkanes elute in order of carbon number: methane first, then ethane, then propane, then butane. Boiling points rise approximately monotonically across the series (roughly −161, −89, −42, and −1°C), and London dispersion interactions with the non-polar stationary phase grow with chain length, so each additional CH₂ unit increases retention time.

# Topic / question being answered

"In what order do methane, ethane, propane, and butane elute from a non-polar GC column, and why does retention time grow with chain length?"
