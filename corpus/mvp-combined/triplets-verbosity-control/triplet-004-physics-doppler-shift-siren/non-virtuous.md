# triplet-004-physics-doppler-shift-siren

For a stationary observer and a sound source moving at v = 30 m/s through still air with sound speed c = 343 m/s, the Doppler-shifted frequency is f = f₀ × c/(c ∓ v), with the minus sign for an approaching source and the plus sign for a receding one. With f₀ = 1000 Hz, the approaching pitch is 1000 × 343/313 ≈ 1096 Hz, and the receding pitch is roughly 1000 × 343/373 ≈ 920 Hz. The approaching shift is about 96 Hz, the receding shift is about 80 Hz. The asymmetry comes from the 1/x relationship between period and frequency: equal source-speed offsets produce unequal frequency-side shifts.
