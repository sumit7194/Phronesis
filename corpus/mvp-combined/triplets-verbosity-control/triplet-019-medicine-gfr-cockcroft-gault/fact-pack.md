# Factual substrate

The Cockcroft-Gault equation estimates creatinine clearance as CrCl = ((140 − age) × weight in kg) / (72 × serum creatinine in mg/dL), with a multiplier of 0.85 for women. For a 60-year-old male with 70 kg body weight and a serum creatinine of 1.0 mg/dL, CrCl ≈ ((140 − 60) × 70) / (72 × 1.0) = 5600 / 72, which is approximately 78 mL/min. Cockcroft-Gault tends to overestimate true GFR somewhat in older or muscular patients.
