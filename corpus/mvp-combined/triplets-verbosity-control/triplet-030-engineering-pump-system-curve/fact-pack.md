# Factual substrate

A centrifugal pump's head-flow curve typically falls with increasing flow, while the system curve (static lift plus friction loss) typically rises with flow. The operating point is the intersection: pump head equals system head and the pump delivers the corresponding flow rate. A pump rated at 30 m head at zero flow and 0 m at 100 m³/h, paired with a system needing 5 m static head plus friction loss that scales as 0.002 × Q², settles near approximately Q ≈ 80 m³/h with a head near 13 m.
