The current data support a commitment to maintaining the policy rate at 4.50% for at least 12 months. Core PCE inflation is 2.1% (3-month annualized) and 2.3% (12-month), both comfortably within the 2% target.  Unemployment remains stable at 4.1%, 0.1 percentage point above our estimate of NAIRU, and has been within 0.2 percentage points of that figure for the past six months. GDP growth is strong at 2.3% annually, matching our projections. The financial conditions index is neutral. 

While the current policy rate is restrictive by 1.0 to 2.0 percentage points based on our estimate of the neutral rate (r*), this uncertainty about the long-term level of the neutral rate is a separate issue, not one that directly affects our decision for the next 12 months. 

The near-term data are consistent and support the current policy stance. The r* debate is a longer-run structural question, not a decision-relevant one for the next 12 months. Therefore, it is prudent to issue forward guidance that reflects this assessment and provides clarity for markets. This would be a prudent approach, acknowledging the unresolved r* uncertainty while remaining confident in the near-term data and the appropriateness of the current policy stance.
