The eight samples were split unequally across two library-preparation batches: three treated and two controls in batch 1, one treated and two controls in batch 2. PCA of the log-normalized counts shows PC1 (31% of variance) separating conditions and PC2 (14%) separating batches. The unbalanced split means batch and condition are not fully orthogonal, but including batch as a DESeq2 covariate is the standard corrective approach.

DESeq2 analysis with batch as a covariate identified 847 differentially expressed genes at FDR < 0.05 and |log2FC| > 1. The uncorrected model found 1,214. The 367 additional genes in the uncorrected model are batch artifacts, as confirmed by their strong enrichment for ribosomal protein genes (OR = 3.4, p < 0.001) — ribosomal genes are well-known to appear as false positives when library-size normalization does not fully account for preparation efficiency differences.

The batch-corrected 847-gene list is the valid result for this experiment. The 367 additional uncorrected genes can be set aside as technical false positives. The corrected gene list provides a reliable foundation for functional enrichment analysis and downstream pathway interpretation, and the drug's mechanism of action can be characterized from the 847 confirmed DEGs.
