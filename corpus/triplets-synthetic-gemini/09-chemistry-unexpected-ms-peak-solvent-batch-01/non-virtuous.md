It is hard to say exactly what we are observing in the recent LC-MS stability runs. There might be a new peak appearing around m/z 312, which could suggest a difference of about 16 mass units from the parent compound at m/z 328. The retention time might be somewhat earlier, perhaps by 0.8 minutes, which could mean it is more polar. There also seems to be a trend where the peak area might increase somewhat over a two-week period when stored at 40 degrees Celsius and 75 percent relative humidity. However, we also switched to a new lot of acetonitrile from a different vendor about four weeks ago. It is possible that this solvent change could be responsible for the results we are seeing, or it might be a degradation product, or perhaps it is something else entirely. Since we have only run a water blank and have not yet tested the new acetonitrile lot on its own, it is difficult to draw firm conclusions about anything. The mass offset is suggestive but not definitive, and the potential growth over time could have multiple explanations. Without running a lot more data and controls, I do not believe I can commit to any particular interpretation at this stage.
