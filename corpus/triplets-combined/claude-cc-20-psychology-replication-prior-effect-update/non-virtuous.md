The base rate at this p-value range is the most directly informative comparator and sits well below half. A direct replication at comparable n is testing whether the true effect is robust enough to clear significance under fresh sampling, and the literature tells us that for studies originally landing in this p-range, that is more often a failing than a passing test. So the prior expectation has to be that replication failure is the modal outcome rather than success.

The lab quality and the moderate effect size shift the picture upward to some degree but do not flip it.
