Accelerated weathering testing of the polyurethane coating under ASTM G154 Cycle 4 conditions demonstrates that the formulation will meet service life requirements for a 20-year outdoor application. Test results at 4,000 hours show gloss retention of 72%, color change ΔE = 3.2, and adhesion 4B, all of which comfortably exceed specification thresholds of 50% gloss, ΔE 5.0 maximum, and 3B adhesion. The margin at 4,000 hours — equivalent to 6.8 years of outdoor service — is sufficiently large to project confidence over the remaining service period.

The validity of the 3× acceleration ratio is confirmed by the natural outdoor exposure panels, which show 88% gloss retention after 3 years, in agreement with the acceleration model. This correlation between the laboratory test and real-world performance validates the methodology and supports the use of the Arrhenius extrapolation for long-term prediction. The projected performance at 20 years falls within the expected service envelope for this coating class based on linear extrapolation of the measured degradation rates.

Based on the combined accelerated and natural exposure data, the coating can be predicted to meet its 20-year outdoor service requirements. The dataset provides a robust and validated basis for this service life prediction, and no further testing is required before proceeding with deployment.
