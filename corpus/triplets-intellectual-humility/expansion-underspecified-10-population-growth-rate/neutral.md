I was asked: "What is the population growth rate?" I need to think about what is required to produce a specific percent-per-year figure.

Population growth rate is a quantity of the form (births − deaths + net migration) divided by the relevant baseline population, computed over a defined window. It is therefore tied to four parameters: the geographic unit (a city, region, country, continent, or the world), the time period over which the rate is computed (annual, decadal, instantaneous), the demographic scope (resident population, citizens-only, age-restricted), and the data source (national statistics agency, UN World Population Prospects, World Bank). Different combinations give materially different numbers.

The four-parameter requirement is the variable bundle that determines whether the requested rate can be produced. I will weigh it against the question's framing before deciding what to write.
