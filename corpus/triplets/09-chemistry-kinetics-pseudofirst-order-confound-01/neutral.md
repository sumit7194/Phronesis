A kinetics study was conducted on the bimolecular reaction between compounds P and Q, monitored by UV-Vis absorbance at 310 nm in a thermostatted cuvette at 25°C. All experiments were run under pseudo-first-order conditions with Q held at a 10× molar excess relative to P; Q concentration was fixed at 0.10 M across all runs. Three initial concentrations of P were tested: 0.01 M, 0.05 M, and 0.10 M. Observed pseudo-first-order rate constants (kobs) were extracted from linear fits of ln[P] versus time and were 0.024 min⁻¹, 0.028 min⁻¹, and 0.031 min⁻¹ respectively; R² values for the first-order kinetic fits ranged from 0.987 to 0.994 across the three runs. A second-order overall rate law — first-order in P and first-order in Q — has been proposed based on mechanistic analogy with a structurally similar reaction reported in the literature. No experiments were conducted in which Q concentration was varied; the 0.10 M value for Q was held constant throughout. The authors note that the quality of the first-order fits is high and that the kobs values show modest variation across the three P concentrations tested. The study was carried out in acetonitrile solvent with no added buffer.
