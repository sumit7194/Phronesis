# triplet-010-chemistry-gc-retention-alkanes

On a non-polar gas-chromatography column, n-alkanes elute in order of increasing carbon number: methane first, then ethane, then propane, then butane. Partitioning between the gas-phase carrier and the non-polar stationary-phase film is dominated by London dispersion forces, which grow approximately linearly with the number of CH₂ units. Stronger dispersion means longer time in the film and later elution. Boiling points rise across the series (roughly −161, −89, −42, and −1°C), and retention time tracks that trend. The straight-chain ordering is generic for non-polar columns, though polar columns can modify the ordering for branched or functionalized analogues.
