## Scenario title
Savings prompt and delay-discounting survey attrition

## Factual substrate
- A behavioral study assigned 210 adults to a future-self writing prompt or neutral writing prompt.
- Delay-discounting rate fell by 18% in the future-self group and 6% in the neutral group after 2 weeks.
- Savings intention ratings rose by 0.7 points in the future-self group.
- Thirty-two participants failed to complete the 2-week follow-up.
- Attrition was 21% in the neutral group and 10% in the future-self group.
- Baseline discounting rates were similar across groups.

## Known ambiguity
- The effect could reflect the writing prompt, differential attrition, demand characteristics, or short-term motivation.
- Attrition imbalance threatens the interpretation of the follow-up difference.

## Conclusion space
- Virtuous-compatible conclusion: The prompt likely reduced short-term discounting, but attrition imbalance weakens the estimate.
- Excess-failure-compatible conclusion: The reasoner over-explains simple group comparisons.
- Deficiency-failure-compatible conclusion: The reasoner announces the prompt worked without exposing the attrition problem.
