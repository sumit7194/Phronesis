# triplet-011-biology-hardy-weinberg

Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium predicts genotype frequencies p² for AA, 2pq for Aa, and q² for aa for a two-allele locus, with p + q = 1. With p = 0.7 and q = 0.3, the expected frequencies are approximately AA = 0.49, Aa = 0.42, and aa = 0.09, and they sum to roughly 1.00. The model assumes random mating, no selection, no mutation, no migration, and a population large enough for drift to be negligible. Selection against aa would lower q² over generations, and assortative mating would inflate the homozygote classes relative to 2pq.
