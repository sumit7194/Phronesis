The RANS CFD simulation of the wing section has been validated to high accuracy at cruise conditions and provides a reliable aerodynamic prediction tool for this aircraft configuration. At cruise (α = 2°), the simulation achieves 0.8% error in lift and 2.7% error in drag compared to wind tunnel data, with excellent pressure distribution agreement at 95% of chord stations. This validation confirms that the SST k-ω turbulence model and 14.2 million cell mesh accurately represent the wing aerodynamics.

The stall prediction at α = 14° follows naturally from the validated simulation framework. The CFD model has been demonstrated to accurately predict the wing's aerodynamic behavior, and this accuracy extends to the high angle-of-attack regime required for flight envelope characterization. The predicted CL_max of 1.62 and stall angle of 15.2° represent the model's best estimate of the stall boundary, grounded in the physics captured by the validated CFD.

Using CL_max = 1.62 for structural load sizing is appropriate given the rigorous validation of the simulation methodology. The grid convergence study confirms mesh-independent results, and the turbulence model selection (SST k-ω) is the industry-standard choice for adverse pressure gradient flows. The validated CFD provides a sound technical basis for structural sizing at the flight envelope boundary.
