# triplet-014-biology-resting-membrane-potential

A typical neuron rests at −70 mV inside relative to outside. Intracellular K⁺ is around 140 mM and extracellular K⁺ around 5 mM; Na⁺ is reversed (12 mM inside, 145 mM outside). At rest the membrane is 50× more permeable to K⁺ than to Na⁺ because K⁺ leak channels are open while voltage-gated Na⁺ channels are closed. That pulls the potential close to E_K ≈ −90 mV; the small Na⁺ permeability and the electrogenic Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase shift it to about −70 mV. The pump moves 3 Na⁺ out and 2 K⁺ in per ATP, sustaining the gradients. The same structure also fits the closely related problems in the field.
