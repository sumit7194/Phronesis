# Factual substrate

The present value of a single future cash flow C received in t years at discount rate r is PV = C / (1 + r)^t. For C = $1000, t = 5 years, and r = 5%, PV = 1000 / 1.05⁵ ≈ 1000 / 1.2763 ≈ $783.53. The discount factor (1 + r)^t reflects the opportunity cost of waiting: $783.53 invested at 5% would compound back to $1000 over five years.

"What is the present value of $1000 to be received in five years at a 5% discount rate, and what does the discount factor represent?"
