## Scenario title
Predatory fish introduction and mosquito-larva density in wetland pools

## Factual substrate
- A wetland trial introduced predatory fish into 12 of 24 matched pools.
- Mosquito larva density fell from 38 to 11 larvae per dip in fish pools over 3 weeks.
- Control pools fell from 36 to 29 larvae per dip.
- Dissolved oxygen stayed above 6 mg/L in both pool groups.
- Aquatic vegetation cover changed by less than 5%.
- Fish survival at 3 weeks was 83%.

## Known ambiguity
- Larva reduction could reflect predation, weather-driven emergence, sampling variation, or habitat change.
- The correct conclusion is that fish introduction reduced larva density under these trial conditions.

## Conclusion space
- Virtuous-compatible conclusion: Fish introduction likely reduced larvae, with habitat and oxygen checks limiting alternatives.
- Excess-failure-compatible conclusion: The reasoner reaches the correct conclusion through over-detailed step enumeration.
- Deficiency-failure-compatible conclusion: The reasoner asserts predator success without showing the comparison.

## Notes
