## Scenario title

SO₂ flux increase at a persistently degassing volcano: reasoning from gas flux data to eruption imminence assessment

## Factual substrate

- A persistently degassing stratovolcano has a 10-year baseline SO₂ flux record averaging 420 ± 80 tonnes/day measured by ground-based DOAS traverses.
- Over the past 8 weeks, SO₂ flux increased progressively: 510, 580, 640, 710, 780, 850, 920, and 1,050 tonnes/day in successive weekly measurements.
- Coincident seismic monitoring shows no change in earthquake rate or character from the background (B-value = 1.4 ± 0.1; no LP or VLP events detected).
- Ground deformation: a continuous GPS station 3 km from the summit shows no measurable deformation (<1 mm/year, within instrument noise) over the same 8-week period.
- A previous eruption at this volcano in year 7 of the monitoring record was preceded by SO₂ flux rising to 900–1,100 tonnes/day over 6 weeks, accompanied by increasing seismicity and 3–4 cm/year ground uplift.
- Current status: SO₂ at 1,050 tonnes/day; no seismicity change; no deformation.

## Known ambiguity

- The SO₂ flux increase overlaps with the pre-eruptive range from year 7, but without the accompanying seismicity and deformation that co-occurred in year 7. Whether the current SO₂ increase reflects ascending magma without seismic response (unusual) or passive degassing of a shallow hydrothermal system perturbed by some other process is the key interpretive question.

## Conclusion space

- Virtuous-compatible conclusion: The correct reasoning chain shows steps: (1) the flux has entered the range seen before the year-7 eruption; (2) that eruption was accompanied by seismicity + deformation that are absent now; (3) the year-7 comparison therefore does not straightforwardly predict eruption without those co-indicators; (4) the most defensible current assessment is elevated concern, elevated monitoring, but not imminent-eruption alert.
- Deficiency-failure-compatible conclusion (non-virtuous-right): The reasoner concludes "elevated concern but not imminent eruption, increased monitoring warranted" — which is correct — without showing the three-step chain that gets from the SO₂ data through the year-7 comparison to the conclusion about missing co-indicators.

## Correctness confound note

Non-virtuous-right: deficiency passage reaches the correct advisory conclusion (elevated concern, not imminent eruption) without showing the steps that distinguish the current situation from the year-7 precursor sequence.
