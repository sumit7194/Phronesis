## Scenario title

A seismologist drafts a 7-day aftershock probability statement following a moderate earthquake on a poorly instrumented fault.

## Factual substrate

- Mainshock magnitude M 5.8 on a previously low-activity strike-slip segment.
- Within 18 hours, 27 aftershocks above the magnitude-of-completeness threshold of M 2.0 have been recorded.
- Empirical Omori-law fit on regional analogs gives expected aftershocks above M 4.0 in the next 7 days at a rate equivalent to a probability of 0.22 of at least one such event.
- Background seismicity rate on this segment is essentially zero over the past two decades.

## Known ambiguity

- The Omori fit relies on regional analogs that may not represent this specific fault segment.
- Whether to communicate a single probability or a range to a public-facing audience.

## Conclusion space

- A: Communicate a probability range with explicit numbers.
- B: Communicate appropriate caution with hedge vocabulary.
- C: Either alarm or false reassure.
