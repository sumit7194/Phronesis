# Factual substrate

The Henderson-Hasselbalch equation is pH = pKa + log([A⁻]/[HA]). For an acetic-acid/acetate buffer with pKa = 4.76 and a conjugate-base-to-acid ratio of [A⁻]/[HA] = 2, the resulting pH is approximately 4.76 + log(2) = 4.76 + 0.30 = 5.06. The buffer resists pH change near pKa because the ratio shifts only slowly when small amounts of strong acid or base are added.
