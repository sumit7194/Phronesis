# triplet-003-physics-snells-law-refraction

Snell's law states n₁ sin θ₁ = n₂ sin θ₂. For light passing from air (n₁ = 1.00) into water (n₂ = approximately 1.33) at an incidence angle of 30°, sin θ₂ = (1.00/1.33) × sin 30° = (1.00/1.33) × 0.5 ≈ 0.376. Therefore the refracted angle is roughly 22.1° from the normal. The ray bends toward the normal because it enters a medium of higher refractive index, which reduces the in-medium phase speed of the wave and pulls the geometric ray direction closer to the perpendicular at the interface.
