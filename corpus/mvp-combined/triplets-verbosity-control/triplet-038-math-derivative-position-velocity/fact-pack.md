# Factual substrate

Velocity is the time derivative of position, and acceleration is the time derivative of velocity. For x(t) = 5t² (in metres, with t in seconds), v(t) = dx/dt = 10t (m/s), and a(t) = dv/dt = 10 (m/s²). The acceleration is constant, so the motion is uniform acceleration starting from rest. At t = 2 s, x = 20 m, v = 20 m/s, and a = 10 m/s².

# Topic / question being answered

"Given x(t) = 5t², what are the velocity and acceleration as functions of time, and what kind of motion does this describe?"
