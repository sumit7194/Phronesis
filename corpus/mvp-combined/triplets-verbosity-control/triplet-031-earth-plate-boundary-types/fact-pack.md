# Factual substrate

There are approximately three types of tectonic plate boundary. Divergent boundaries (e.g., the Mid-Atlantic Ridge) feature plates moving apart, with new ocean crust formed by sea-floor spreading. Convergent boundaries (e.g., the Andes) feature plates moving together, with one plate subducting under another, producing volcanic arcs and deep trenches. Transform boundaries (e.g., the San Andreas Fault) feature plates sliding laterally past each other, producing strike-slip earthquakes without significant crust creation or destruction.
