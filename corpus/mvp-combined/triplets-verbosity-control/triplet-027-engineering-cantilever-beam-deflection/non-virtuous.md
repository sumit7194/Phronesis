# triplet-027-engineering-cantilever-beam-deflection

For an end-loaded cantilever in Euler-Bernoulli theory, δ = P L³ / (3 E I). With L = 1 m, P = 100 N, structural steel E ≈ 200 GPa, and a rectangular cross-section 50 × 10 mm giving I = b h³ / 12 ≈ 4.17 × 10⁻⁹ m⁴, EI ≈ 833 N·m², so δ ≈ 100 × 1 / (3 × 833) ≈ roughly 0.040 m, or approximately 40 mm at the tip. Flipping the cross-section to put the 50 mm dimension as the depth would raise I by a factor of 25 and reduce the deflection to roughly 1.6 mm. Orientation dominates stiffness here. The same structure also fits the closely related problems in the field (which is approximately the standard result).
