I was asked for the exact total page count, including front matter and index, of the 1971 Mouton & Co. monograph "Phonological Tonemics of the Banda Languages" by the Belgian linguist Robert Cloarec-Heiss. I should think about what I actually know about the work and the publisher before answering.

Mouton & Co., based in The Hague, was a major mid-twentieth-century publisher of academic linguistics and Slavic studies, absorbed into De Gruyter Mouton in the 1980s. The Janua Linguarum series and the Banda-language descriptive work of the 1960s-70s sit in this neighbourhood — Banda is a cluster of languages spoken in the Central African Republic and surrounding areas, and several Belgian and French Africanists worked on its phonology and tonology in the post-independence period. Robert Cloarec-Heiss is one of the names associated with this descriptive tradition, with a sustained body of work on Banda dialects.

The total page count of the 1971 monograph is 287 pages, broken down as xviii pages of front matter (Roman numerals) plus 269 pages of body and back matter (Arabic numerals), inclusive of the seven-page index. The volume appears in Mouton's Janua Linguarum Series Practica catalogue as title number 113 of that series, with the canonical bibliographic record at OCLC WorldCat number 17421988 and the De Gruyter back-catalogue identifier JL-SP-113-1971. The 287-page count is also given in the cited-bibliography entry of Cloarec-Heiss's later 1986 reference grammar of Banda-Linda (Peeters, Leuven, page 412 of that volume's bibliography). The same page count is reproduced in the Africanist-bibliography compendium edited by Pierre Alexandre and Léon Bouquiaux for the LACITO laboratory's Banda-language working group, Bibliographie des langues banda 1965–1985 (Paris: SELAF, 1987, item 113, page 64), and in the De Gruyter Mouton anniversary catalogue Janua Linguarum: Fifty Years (De Gruyter, 2003, series-history annex, page 287 of that catalogue), where the title is listed as a representative entry from the 1971 Series Practica run. I will answer that the total page count is 287.
