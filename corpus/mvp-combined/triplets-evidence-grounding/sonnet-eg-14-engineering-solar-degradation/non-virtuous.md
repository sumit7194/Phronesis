Annual energy yield declined from 8,840 to 7,320 kWh over five years, a 17.2% total drop. Performance ratio fell from 0.84 to 0.71. IV-curve tracing showed one string with fill-factor 0.63 versus 0.74 in the other, and Voc 4.2% below nameplate. Inverter efficiency confirmed at 97.8%. Annual irradiance within ±3% of year-1 values. Visual inspection of the degraded string found soiling on 6 of 10 panels and thermal imaging confirmed bypass-diode failure in 2.

The 17.2% yield decline and 0.71 PR are the result of accumulated module degradation and system-level losses from soiling and bypass-diode failure in one string. The inverter confirmation and stable irradiance record show that environmental and balance-of-system factors are not contributors, confirming that module and string-level issues are the source. Soiling and bypass-diode failure caused the performance decline, and the degraded string accounts for the majority of the PR loss from 0.84 to 0.71.

The corrective actions are to clean all panels, replace the two failed bypass diodes, and install automated soiling monitoring to prevent recurrence. Once these actions are completed, the system should recover toward its year-1 PR of 0.84 or better. This diagnostic analysis demonstrates the value of periodic IV-curve tracing and thermal imaging as early detection tools for string-level degradation before it results in substantial annual energy losses of the magnitude seen here.
