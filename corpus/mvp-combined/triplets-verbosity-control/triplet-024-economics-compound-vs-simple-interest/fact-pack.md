# Factual substrate

A $1000 principal earning a 5% annual rate over 10 years yields $1500 under simple interest (1000 + 10 × 50 = 1500) and approximately $1628.89 under annual compounding (1000 × 1.05¹⁰ ≈ 1628.89). The compound balance exceeds the simple balance by roughly $128.89 over this horizon, and the gap grows nonlinearly with both rate and time.

# Topic / question being answered

"How much does $1000 grow to over 10 years at a 5% annual rate under simple interest versus compound interest, and why does the gap widen with longer horizons?"
