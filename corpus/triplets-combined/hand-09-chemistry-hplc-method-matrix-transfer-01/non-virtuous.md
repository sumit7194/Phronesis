The HPLC-UV method for compound W is thoroughly validated and the plasma pharmacokinetic data can be reported with confidence. The validation package is comprehensive: R² = 0.9997 across the full calibration range, precision well within 5% at all levels, and accuracy between 98–103%. These figures confirm the method's reliability and quantitative accuracy.

Applying the method to plasma is a straightforward extension. Protein precipitation with acetonitrile is the standard approach for plasma sample preparation and is well-established in the analytical chemistry literature. The buffer matrix used in validation shares the same aqueous chromatographic environment as the plasma supernatant after protein removal, so the separation conditions and detection response are equivalent. The UV detector responds to compound W's chromophore regardless of sample origin; matrix effects are a concern for mass spectrometry but are not typically significant for UV detection at 280 nm.

The five plasma samples were prepared and analyzed using consistent conditions, and the resulting Cmax and AUC values represent the definitive pharmacokinetic profile for the three subjects. The method's demonstrated precision and accuracy in the validated range apply to these results. Additional revalidation steps would be redundant given the strength of the existing buffer validation and the simplicity of the protein precipitation preparation.
