# Factual substrate

The Mohs scale ranks ten reference minerals by scratch hardness on an ordinal scale: talc (1), gypsum (2), calcite (3), fluorite (4), apatite (5), orthoclase feldspar (6), quartz (7), topaz (8), corundum (9), and diamond (10). A mineral with higher Mohs number scratches one of equal or lower number. Quartz at 7 scratches feldspar at 6 but is itself scratched by topaz at 8. The scale is ordinal, not linear: the absolute hardness of diamond is roughly 4 times that of corundum, despite being only one Mohs step higher.
