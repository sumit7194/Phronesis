TOFD inspection found four indications: two at the cap/root interface (2.1 and 3.4 mm depth), one mid-wall (12.2 mm), and one near the inner surface (22.8 mm). All four are characterized as planar. RT detected two indications — a porosity cluster at approximately 8 mm and an elongated mid-wall indication consistent with the TOFD mid-wall finding. The two cap/root indications were not detected by RT.

TOFD sized the mid-wall indication at 4.3 mm through-wall height and 31 mm length. RT provided no reliable sizing for this planar flaw — conventional radiography is a detection technique for volumetric flaws and is not a sizing method for planar discontinuities. The fitness-for-service assessment requires flaw height and length as the primary inputs; it does not require flaw-type identification (slag, lack of fusion, crack) as a precondition to the fracture mechanics calculation.

The TOFD results supply the dimensional inputs the FFS calculation requires: 4.3 mm height and 31 mm length for the mid-wall indication, characterized as planar. The RT result independently confirms detection of the mid-wall flaw and adds the porosity cluster, which TOFD also detected by characterization category. The two cap/root indications exist in the TOFD record and require separate FFS evaluation at their respective depths and dimensions. The appropriate next step is to run the FFS assessment with the TOFD dimensions and determine whether each indication is tolerable at the required service conditions.
