# Factual substrate

Under Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium, the genotype frequencies for a two-allele locus are p² for AA, 2pq for Aa, and q² for aa, where p and q are the allele frequencies and p + q = 1. With p = 0.7 and q = 0.3, the expected genotype frequencies are approximately AA = 0.49, Aa = 0.42, and aa = 0.09. The model assumes random mating, no selection, no mutation, no migration, and a population large enough that drift is negligible.

"What genotype frequencies does Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium predict for a two-allele locus with p = 0.7 and q = 0.3, and which assumptions must hold for that prediction?"
