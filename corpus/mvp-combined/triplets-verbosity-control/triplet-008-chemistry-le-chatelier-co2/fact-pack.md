# Factual substrate

The dissolution and dissociation chain CO₂(g) + H₂O ⇌ H₂CO₃ ⇌ H⁺ + HCO₃⁻ links atmospheric CO₂ to seawater pH. Raising pCO₂ from approximately 400 to 1000 ppm by Le Chatelier's principle shifts the chain to the right, generating more H₂CO₃ and more H⁺, which lowers the seawater pH. The total dissolved inorganic carbon increases, and the carbonate-ion concentration decreases somewhat as the bicarbonate side grows.
