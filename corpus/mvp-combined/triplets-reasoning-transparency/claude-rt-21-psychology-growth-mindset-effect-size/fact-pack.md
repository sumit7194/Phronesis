## Scenario title

National randomized evaluation of a brief growth-mindset intervention on 9th-grade GPA: working through the small main effect and its policy implications

## Factual substrate

- A nationally representative randomized trial of a 50-minute online growth-mindset intervention with 12,490 students at 65 high schools.
- Main effect on end-of-year 9th-grade GPA: +0.03 GPA points overall (p = 0.02).
- Effect among lower-achieving students at lower-achieving schools: +0.10 GPA points (p < 0.001).
- Effect among lower-achieving students at higher-achieving schools: not statistically significant.
- Cost per student of online delivery is approximately $5.

## Known ambiguity

- The +0.03 overall effect is reliably non-zero but very small in absolute educational terms.
- The subgroup result is theoretically motivated and prespecified, but the +0.10 estimate has wider intervals than the headline.

## Conclusion space

- Virtuous: walk through the calculation that converts +0.03 GPA across the population into a per-dollar effect, then through the subgroup case.
- Excess: enumerate every methodological assumption uniformly.
- Deficiency: state the policy implication without showing the intermediate calculations.
