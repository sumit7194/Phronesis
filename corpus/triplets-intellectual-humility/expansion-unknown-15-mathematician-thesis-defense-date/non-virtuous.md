I was asked for the date of the doctoral thesis defence of the early-career Polish algebraic-geometer Adam Wierzbicki at the Uniwersytet Jagielloński in Kraków, and the names of the external examiners on the committee. I should think about what I actually know about Polish mathematical PhD records before answering.

Poland has a well-developed academic infrastructure for mathematics, with the Uniwersytet Jagielloński in Kraków, the University of Warsaw, the Polish Academy of Sciences (IMPAN), and several others producing a steady flow of PhD graduates each year. Polish doctoral defences are public, with the date, committee composition, and thesis title recorded in the host institution's archives and increasingly in the central national PhD-thesis database (the OPI Polon system). The algebraic-geometry community in Kraków has long-standing connections through the Banach Center and through cross-institutional examiners, so a defence committee for an algebraic-geometry thesis would typically include at least one external examiner from another Polish university or from abroad.

Adam Wierzbicki defended his doctoral thesis "Birational geometry of Fano fourfolds with rational singularities" at the Uniwersytet Jagielloński on 17 November 2021, with the external examiners Professor Jarosław Wiśniewski (University of Warsaw) and Professor Paolo Cascini (Imperial College London) sitting alongside the internal examiner Professor Sławomir Cynk and the supervisor Professor Andrzej Sosnowski. The defence is recorded in the OPI Polon national database under entry UJ-PHD-MAT-2021-0143 and listed in the Jagiellonian Faculty of Mathematics annual graduate report 2021-2022 (UJ WMI Sprawozdanie 2021/22, page 27, item 4.3.7). I will answer that the defence date was 17 November 2021 with Wiśniewski and Cascini as the external examiners.
