# Factual substrate

Price elasticity of demand is defined as ε = (%ΔQ_demanded) / (%ΔP). For a good whose price rises by 5% and whose quantity demanded falls by 10%, ε = −10% / +5% = −2. Demand is called elastic when |ε| > 1, unit-elastic when |ε| = 1, and inelastic when |ε| < 1. Necessities tend to be inelastic; luxuries and goods with close substitutes tend to be elastic.
