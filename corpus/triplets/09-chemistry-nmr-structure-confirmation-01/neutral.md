Compound A was synthesized in two steps from commercially available starting material B using a well-precedented reaction pathway. The structure was characterized by ¹H NMR (CDCl₃, 400 MHz), ¹³C NMR, DEPT-135, low-resolution mass spectrometry, and HPLC purity analysis.

¹H NMR showed all 8 expected proton environments. Chemical shifts were consistent with literature analogs within Δδ < 0.1 ppm, integration ratios matched the molecular formula exactly, and coupling constants were consistent with the predicted stereochemical geometry. ¹³C NMR showed all 12 expected carbon environments with shifts consistent with the proposed connectivity. DEPT-135 confirmed CH, CH₂, and CH₃ assignments for every carbon environment as predicted. Low-resolution mass spectrometry gave m/z = 287.1 [M+H]⁺ (calculated 287.1) consistent with molecular formula C₁₅H₂₂N₂O₃. HPLC purity was 99.2% by UV area at 254 nm, with a single sharp peak and baseline separation from all other peaks.

No unexpected side products have been observed in prior runs of this synthetic sequence. High-resolution mass spectrometry and X-ray crystallography were not obtained as part of this characterization package.
