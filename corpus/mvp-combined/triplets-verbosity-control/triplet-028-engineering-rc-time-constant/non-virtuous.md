# triplet-028-engineering-rc-time-constant

For a series RC circuit charged from a step supply, V_C(t) = V_s × (1 − e^(−t/τ)) with τ = RC. With R = 1 kΩ and C = 100 μF, τ = 1000 × 100 × 10⁻⁶ = 0.1 s. The capacitor reaches roughly 63.2% of V_s at t = τ, 86.5% at 2τ, 95.0% at 3τ, 98.2% at 4τ, and approximately 99.3% at 5τ. The 5τ rule of thumb gives "essentially full charge" at about 0.5 s. Discharging follows the same time constant: an exponential decay over τ and to zero by 5τ. The same structure also fits the closely related problems in the field (which is approximately the standard result).
