This is close to a best-case phylogenetic evidence package for a contentious taxon. The analysis uses substantial nuclear data (73 genes, ~84,000 bp) across 68 taxa, and four frameworks with different optimality criteria (IQ-TREE2 maximum likelihood, MrBayes Bayesian, ASTRAL-III coalescent species tree, and TNT parsimony) independently recover the same placement: within Malpighiales, sister to Violaceae. Support values are at or near the ceiling (bootstrap ≥ 98%, posterior probability 1.00, ASTRAL local PP 0.98).

I have high confidence in the topological conclusion because agreement across methods reduces the chance that the result is an artifact of one model class, and the study directly tested two major confounds. Long-branch attraction was probed by pruning and re-analysis with reduced taxon sets, and compositional bias was checked with GC homogeneity tests showing no significant deviation in the focal lineage. The historical morphology-based proposals (Aristolochiales, Laurales) are not just “different,” they are actively rejected by all four approaches with high support.

I have lower confidence about ancillary claims like divergence times or whether rare processes (e.g., host-associated transfers) occur, because those are not fully addressed here and can be hard to exclude categorically. But those uncertainties are not symmetrical with the placement itself. Calibrated bottom line: the Malpighiales sister-to-Violaceae placement is well supported and can be treated as the current best understanding.
