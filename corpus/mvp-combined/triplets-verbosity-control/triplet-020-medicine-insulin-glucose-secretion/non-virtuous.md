# triplet-020-medicine-insulin-glucose-secretion

In a pancreatic β-cell, glucose enters via GLUT2, glucokinase phosphorylates it, glycolysis and mitochondrial oxidation raise the ATP/ADP ratio, ATP-sensitive K⁺ channels close, the membrane depolarizes, voltage-gated Ca²⁺ channels open, and intracellular Ca²⁺ triggers insulin-granule exocytosis. At fasting glucose of 80-90 mg/dL the basal insulin level is roughly 10 μU/mL, and a postprandial rise to 140-180 mg/dL drives a roughly 10-fold increase in secretion. The chain encodes plasma glucose into insulin output in approximately real time, and the GLUT2-glucokinase pair sets the responsive concentration range. The same structure also fits the closely related problems in the field.
