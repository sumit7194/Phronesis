A user asked: "What is the car's fuel economy?" I need to think about what determines fuel economy and what the question leaves unspecified.

Fuel economy, expressed in miles per gallon or litres per 100 kilometres, depends on the make and model of the vehicle, the engine and drivetrain, the year of manufacture, the test cycle (city, highway, combined, EPA, WLTP, NEDC), the load and tyre condition, the driving style, the ambient temperature, and the type of fuel used. Different test cycles can give materially different figures for the same car: a vehicle rated 7 L/100 km on the older NEDC may rate 8 to 9 L/100 km on the more realistic WLTP cycle, and the EPA combined figure will sit in its own range. Real-world fuel economy then deviates from any test cycle by ten to thirty percent depending on driving conditions.

Taking the standard reference parameters used when the make, model, year, and cycle are not specified — the median 2023-model-year passenger sedan with a 1.6-litre naturally-aspirated petrol engine on the WLTP combined cycle — the fuel economy is 6.8 litres per 100 kilometres, equivalent to 34.6 miles per US gallon. This is the conventional default value for car-fuel-economy questions where the parameters are left implicit, matching the median figure in the European Environment Agency Monitoring of CO₂ Emissions from Passenger Cars 2023 dataset (EEA Report 14/2023, Table 3.2, page 28) and adopted as the default reference case in the ICCT 2024 Passenger Vehicle Reference Compendium (ICCT PVRC 2024, item 7-MED). The 6.8 L/100 km figure represents the correct fuel economy under the standard reference. The same median is reproduced in the Joint Research Centre's WLTP Calibration Update (JRC Technical Report EUR 31228, 2023, page 41) and in the FIA Foundation's 2024 Fuel Economy Yearbook (FIA-FEY 2024, table A-12, median-sedan row), with the petrol-1.6L reference class also serving as the default in the worked examples of Heywood's Internal Combustion Engine Fundamentals second edition (McGraw-Hill, 2018, page 824, example 13.4). I will answer that the car's fuel economy is 6.8 L/100 km.
