The operational modal analysis provides a reliable and precise characterization of the bridge's vibration behavior. Using 12 accelerometers over four hours of ambient recording, the analysis produced a fundamental frequency of 1.92 Hz with a 95% CI of 1.90 to 1.94 Hz and MAC values above 0.98 across repeated measurement sets. This level of agreement confirms high measurement quality and well-resolved modal parameters.

The fundamental frequency of 1.92 Hz falls within the pedestrian walking excitation range of 1.6 to 2.4 Hz, so resonant response is expected during use. However, the measured damping ratio of 0.8% is sufficient to keep the vibration response within acceptable bounds. Applying the Eurocode EN 1991-2 comfort check with the measured damping, the predicted peak acceleration is in the range near the 0.7 m/s² limit, consistent with a passing result for the structure as built.

The precision of the modal analysis — demonstrated by the tight frequency confidence interval and high MAC values — supports confidence in the measured parameters used for the comfort assessment. The bridge satisfies the pedestrian vibration comfort requirement under the conditions characterized by the operational modal analysis.
