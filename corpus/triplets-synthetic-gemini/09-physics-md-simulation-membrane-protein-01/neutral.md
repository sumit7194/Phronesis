Evaluating structural coordinates, this state generated an explicit 4.3 Å RMSD shift tracking the VSD, measuring a smaller 1.8 Å variance tracking the activation gate. Although existing verified cryo-EM models capture distinct standard open and closed physical structures, absolutely zero available experimental crystallography currently confirms this unique intermediate topology physically exists.
