## Scenario title
Tension between measured and theory-predicted muon anomalous magnetic moment

## Factual substrate
- The combined experimental measurement of the muon anomalous magnetic moment is aμ(exp) = 116592061(41) × 10⁻¹¹.
- The data-driven Standard Model prediction using e⁺e⁻ → hadrons inputs gives aμ(SM, dispersive) = 116591810(43) × 10⁻¹¹, leaving a 4.2σ deviation.
- A lattice-QCD calculation by an independent collaboration gives the hadronic vacuum polarisation contribution that, when used in place of the dispersive value, brings aμ(SM, lattice) into 1.5σ agreement with experiment.
- The two SM predictions disagree by ~2.5σ on the hadronic vacuum polarisation piece itself.
- Beam-dynamics systematics in the experiment are budgeted at 56 ppb out of a 217 ppb total combined uncertainty.
- A new e⁺e⁻ → π⁺π⁻ cross-section measurement has appeared that disagrees with prior measurements by ~3σ in the dominant low-energy region.

## Known ambiguity
- Whether the deviation is new physics, lattice/dispersive theory disagreement, or the new cross-section measurement being correct.

## Conclusion space
- Virtuous: Distinguish the experimental measurement from two different theoretical predictions, and from the new hadronic cross-section measurement, treating each as a different evidence type.
- Deficiency: Asserts new physics from a 4.2σ tension without naming the lattice/dispersive disagreement, the new cross-section result, or the budget split.
- Excess: Bureaucratic citation density.
