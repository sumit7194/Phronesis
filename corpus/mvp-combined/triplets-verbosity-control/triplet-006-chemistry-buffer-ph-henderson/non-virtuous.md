# triplet-006-chemistry-buffer-ph-henderson

The Henderson-Hasselbalch equation gives pH = pKa + log([A⁻]/[HA]). For an acetic-acid/acetate buffer with pKa approximately 4.76 and a 2:1 ratio of conjugate base to acid, pH = 4.76 + log(2) = 4.76 + roughly 0.30 = 5.06. Only the ratio enters the calculation; absolute concentrations affect buffer capacity but not pH. The buffer is most effective near pKa because at [A⁻]/[HA] = 1 the log term vanishes, and small ratio shifts produce small log shifts on either side. The result is approximately temperature-dependent through pKa. The same structure also fits the closely related problems in the field (which is approximately the standard result).
