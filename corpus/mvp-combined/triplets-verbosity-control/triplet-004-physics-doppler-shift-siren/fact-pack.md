# Factual substrate

For a stationary observer and a sound source of frequency f₀ = 1000 Hz moving through still air with sound speed c = 343 m/s and source speed v = 30 m/s, the perceived frequencies are f = f₀·c/(c − v) ≈ 1096 Hz when the source approaches and f = f₀·c/(c + v) ≈ 920 Hz when the source recedes. The pitch shift is roughly +96 Hz approaching and −80 Hz receding.

# Topic / question being answered

"What pitch does a stationary observer hear when a 1000-Hz siren passes them at 30 m/s, and why does the approaching shift differ in magnitude from the receding shift?"
