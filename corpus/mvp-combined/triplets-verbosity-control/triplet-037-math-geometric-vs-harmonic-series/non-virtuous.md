# triplet-037-math-geometric-vs-harmonic-series

The geometric series Σ_{n=0}^∞ (1/2)^n converges to 2: its partial sum is 2 − (1/2)^N, which approximately approaches 2 as N → ∞. The harmonic series Σ_{n=1}^∞ (1/n) diverges, even though its terms vanish, because partial sums grow roughly like ln(N). The ratio test takes the limit of |a_{n+1}/a_n|: for the geometric series this is 1/2, broadly indicating absolute convergence; for the harmonic series it is 1, which leaves the test inconclusive. The integral test then compares Σ(1/n) to ∫(1/x)dx = ln(x) and generally confirms divergence. Vanishing terms are necessary for convergence but not sufficient; the rate matters.
