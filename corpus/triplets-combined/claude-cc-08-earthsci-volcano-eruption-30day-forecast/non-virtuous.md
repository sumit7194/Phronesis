Indicator state: volcano-tectonic earthquake rate has gone from a baseline of around 4 per week to 38 per week, SO2 flux from 80 t/d background to 410 t/d, summit inflation around 24 mm over six weeks. The analog set has 9 of 14 historical sequences at this indicator combination going on to erupt within 60 days while 5 did not.

The volcano is clearly in elevated unrest. Eruption over the next 30 days is more likely than not in the qualitative sense, but the data are not strong enough to rule out the volcano settling back without an eruption either. The analog set of 14 is small and the cases inside it are heterogeneous, so I would not want to lean hard on any specific number drawn from that comparison.

Conditional on indicators continuing to escalate over the coming week, my view shifts toward eruption being the more likely outcome by a comfortable margin within the 30-day window. Conditional on a plateau, the picture is genuinely close to a coin flip, with eruption still somewhat the more likely outcome but not by much. If indicators retreat toward background, the 30-day eruption case becomes the less likely outcome, though not so unlikely that we could relax the precautionary footing.

For the civil authorities, the right communication is that the 30-day eruption probability is meaningfully elevated, that it is not so high that eruption should be treated as essentially expected, and that the indicators warrant the precautionary measures already on the table. I am reasonably confident that this framing matches what the data and the analog comparison support, with the caveat that small-sample inputs leave room for the picture to shift either way.
