The current data unequivocally demonstrate the need to maintain the policy rate at 4.50% for at least 12 months. Core PCE inflation, at 2.1% (3-month annualized) and 2.3% (12-month), clearly establishes our commitment to achieving the 2% target. Unemployment remains stable at 4.1%, definitively showing that we are on track to reach our NAIRU target.  GDP growth is robust at 2.3% annually, confirming our projections. The financial conditions index is neutral, confirming our policy stance.

While the current policy rate is restrictive by 1.0 to 2.0 percentage points, this is a minor technicality. The debate about the long-term neutral rate is irrelevant to our decision for the next 12 months. The near-term data are conclusive, providing a clear path forward.  We are confident in this assessment, and our forward guidance will reflect this.  This forward guidance, based on the current data, provides clarity and certainty for markets.  The r* debate is a long-term issue, but our focus is on the immediate future.
