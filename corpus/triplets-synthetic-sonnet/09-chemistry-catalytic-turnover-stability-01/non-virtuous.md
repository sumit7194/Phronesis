The palladium catalyst (3 wt% Pd/γ-Al₂O₃) demonstrated excellent performance in continuous-flow hydrogenation of the aromatic nitro compound. Initial turnover frequency of 2,400 h⁻¹ with 97.2% selectivity and 99.1% conversion over the first four hours establishes this as a highly active and selective catalyst for this transformation. Performance over four to eight hours showed TOF at 1,800 h⁻¹ with selectivity maintained at 96.8% at 98.5% conversion, confirming that both activity and selectivity remain strong throughout the test period.

Post-run characterization identified some changes in the catalyst: a modest increase in Pd particle size from 3.2 to 4.1 nm, a shift in the Pd²⁺/Pd⁰ ratio from 0.3 to 0.5, and trace Pd detected in the product stream at 12 ppb. These are consistent with normal catalyst evolution under continuous reaction conditions and do not represent significant deactivation at this stage of operation.

The catalyst's performance profile — high initial TOF, maintained selectivity through eight hours, and activity that remains well above minimum process requirements even after the slight decline — supports its suitability for the 500-hour manufacturing campaign. The excellent selectivity of 97% is particularly important for product quality in this application, and the catalyst delivers this consistently. With an initial TOF of 2,400 h⁻¹ and a sustained TOF of 1,800 h⁻¹ still meeting process targets, this catalyst is well-suited for deployment in continuous manufacturing.
