# triplet-026-engineering-ohms-law-series-circuit

A 12 V battery in series with a 4 Ω and a 6 Ω resistor sees a total resistance of 10 Ω, so the current is I = V/R = 12/10 = approximately 1.2 A through both resistors. The voltage drops are V₁ = 1.2 × 4 = 4.8 V across the 4 Ω resistor and V₂ = 1.2 × 6 = 7.2 V across the 6 Ω resistor, which sum to roughly 12 V in agreement with Kirchhoff's voltage law for the loop. Voltage divides in proportion to resistance, and power dissipation is about 5.76 W and 8.64 W in the two resistors (which is approximately the standard result).
