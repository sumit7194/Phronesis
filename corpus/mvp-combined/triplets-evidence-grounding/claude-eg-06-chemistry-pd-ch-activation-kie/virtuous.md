Two different kinds of claim are mixed in the mechanistic story and they need to be kept apart.

Empirically, three numbers are direct measurements. The parallel-reaction KIE on d₅-substrate at 25% conversion gave kH/kD = 4.2 ± 0.3. The Hammett ρ across five para-substituted substrates is −0.91 with R² = 0.96. The stoichiometric palladacycle transferred 92% of the metallated aryl in 30 min. Conversion of the catalytic reaction at 100 °C, 6 h is 84%. These are observed quantities tied to the substrate, conditions, and isotopologue specified.

The DFT result — a 24.3 kcal/mol transition state with a CMD-like geometry and a computed kH/kD of 4.7 at 373 K at B3LYP-D3/6-31G(d), LANL2DZ on Pd — is a model output, not a measurement. It is a calculation under a chosen functional, basis set, and effective core potential, applied to a chosen mechanistic guess. Its agreement with the measured KIE is supportive but does not by itself rule out alternatives.

What the empirical numbers actually anchor is rate-limiting C–H cleavage with electrophilic character at the ipso carbon. The 4.2 KIE is consistent with rate-limiting cleavage; the negative ρ of −0.91 is consistent with positive charge accumulation in the rate-limiting step. The 92% palladacycle transfer in 30 min anchors the palladacycle as kinetically competent on-cycle, separate from any geometry choice.

The mechanism claim that the cleavage is specifically CMD with carboxylate-assisted concerted geometry is partly empirical (KIE, ρ shape) and partly computational (the 24.3 kcal/mol path with that geometry). A pre-equilibrium isotope-sensitive coordination can also yield kH/kD near 4 in this regime, so the geometry assignment rests more on the model than on the data.
