## Scenario title
Reconstructing late-Holocene monsoon strength from a single cave speleothem

## Factual substrate
- A 38 cm aragonite stalagmite from a low-altitude cave was sub-sampled at 0.5 mm resolution for δ¹⁸O.
- U–Th dating at 12 horizons gave an age model spanning 2,100 to 200 years BP with 2σ uncertainties of ±15–40 years.
- Mean δ¹⁸O over the record is −7.4‰ VPDB; a 0.9‰ negative excursion centred at 1,150 years BP lasts 60 years.
- Modern cave monitoring shows drip-water δ¹⁸O within 0.3‰ of local meteoric water; cave temperature is 22.4 ± 0.2 °C.
- A regional pollen record from a lake 80 km away shows a tree-pollen rise centred at 1,200 ± 50 years BP.
- A regional speleothem network of 6 other caves has 4 records showing concurrent negative δ¹⁸O excursions of 0.4–1.1‰ in the same window.

## Known ambiguity
- δ¹⁸O in monsoon caves can reflect rainfall amount, source-region temperature, or moisture-source change.
- Single-record interpretation is observational, not experimental.

## Conclusion space
- Virtuous: Distinguish proxy measurement (δ¹⁸O, U–Th), monitoring calibration, regional comparison, and cross-proxy comparison as distinct evidence types.
- Excess: Wrap the same numbers in citation-density to standard speleothem and U–Th protocols.
- Deficiency: Strip the U–Th uncertainties, the 0.9‰ excursion size, the regional network counts.
