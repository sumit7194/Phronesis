Water samples drawn from a municipal monitoring site downstream of an industrial chemical outfall were processed via LC-MS/MS. The overarching analysis screened a comprehensive panel targeting 84 discrete environmental contaminants. During the protocol, one specific sample isolated a positive analytical identification for compound Q, a regulated chlorinated phenolic. 

The identification relies upon three converging data streams. First, the generated precursor ion mass at m/z 161.0 [M-H]⁻ aligns with the predicted molecular weight of compound Q. Second, two primary product ions at m/z 125.0 and m/z 97.0 map to the standard library fragmentation pattern, generating a dot-product spectral match score of 0.94 (exceeding the standard 0.85 positive identification threshold). Third, the chromatographic retention time recorded at 8.42 minutes matches the authentic analytical standard deployed at 8.44 minutes, satisfying the stringent ±0.05 minute tolerance window. The external 6-point calibration curve quantified the concentration at 0.34 μg/L, which sits below the 0.50 μg/L regulatory action level. Method blanks were clean, and the matrix spike successfully recovered at 94%. This specific operational workflow directly adheres to EPA Method 537.1 Level 1 environmental evaluation standards. The data output is currently logged for regulatory review.
