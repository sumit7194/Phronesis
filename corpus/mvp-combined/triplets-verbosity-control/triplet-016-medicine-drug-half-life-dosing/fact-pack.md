# Factual substrate

For a drug with first-order elimination and a half-life of approximately 8 hours, plasma concentrations approach steady state after roughly 5 half-lives, or about 40 hours of regular dosing. A dosing interval near one half-life keeps peak-to-trough fluctuation within roughly a factor of two; longer intervals produce wider fluctuation, while shorter intervals produce narrower fluctuation but more frequent administration.

# Topic / question being answered

"How long does it take a drug with an 8-hour half-life to reach steady state, and how does the dosing interval affect peak-to-trough fluctuation?"
