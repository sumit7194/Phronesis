# Factual substrate

For an end-loaded cantilever in Euler-Bernoulli theory, the tip deflection is δ = PL³/(3EI), where P is the tip load, L is the length, E is Young's modulus, and I is the cross-sectional second moment of area. With a 1 m steel beam (E ≈ 200 GPa), a rectangular cross-section 50 mm × 10 mm (I = bh³/12 ≈ 4.17 × 10⁻⁹ m⁴), and a 100 N tip load, δ ≈ 100 × 1³ / (3 × 200 × 10⁹ × 4.17 × 10⁻⁹) ≈ 0.040 m, or about 40 mm.
