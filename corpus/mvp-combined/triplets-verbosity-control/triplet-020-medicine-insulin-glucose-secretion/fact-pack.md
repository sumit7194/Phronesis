# Factual substrate

In pancreatic β-cells, glucose entry through the GLUT2 transporter and phosphorylation by glucokinase raises the intracellular ATP/ADP ratio, which closes ATP-sensitive K⁺ channels (K_ATP). Membrane depolarization opens voltage-gated Ca²⁺ channels, intracellular Ca²⁺ rises, and insulin granules undergo exocytosis. Fasting blood glucose of approximately 80-90 mg/dL drives a low basal insulin level near 10 μU/mL; a postprandial rise to 140-180 mg/dL drives a roughly 10-fold increase in insulin secretion.
