# Factual substrate

The solubility product for barium sulfate is approximately Ksp = 1.1 × 10⁻¹⁰ at 25°C. For the equilibrium BaSO₄(s) ⇌ Ba²⁺(aq) + SO₄²⁻(aq), the molar solubility in pure water is s, with [Ba²⁺] = [SO₄²⁻] = s, so s² = Ksp and s = √(1.1 × 10⁻¹⁰) ≈ 1.05 × 10⁻⁵ M. In the presence of a common ion, solubility drops sharply.
