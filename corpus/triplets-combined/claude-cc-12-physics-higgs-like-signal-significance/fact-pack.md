## Scenario title

A particle physicist evaluates a 3.4σ excess in a heavy-flavor decay channel against the standard model background.

## Factual substrate

- Excess observed at invariant mass 4,150 MeV in a heavy-flavor decay channel.
- Local significance after look-elsewhere correction estimated at 2.2σ.
- Local significance before correction quoted at 3.4σ.
- Independent partner experiment has 0.6σ excess at the same mass (consistent with background).

## Known ambiguity

- The discrepancy between 3.4σ local and 2.2σ global is the kind of statistical fluctuation that resolves with more data.
- The independent experiment's null result reduces the posterior on a real new state.

## Conclusion space

- A: Posterior probability quantified, not a discovery, follow-up runs warranted.
- B: Same picture qualitatively.
- C: Either treat as discovery or dismiss without quantification.
