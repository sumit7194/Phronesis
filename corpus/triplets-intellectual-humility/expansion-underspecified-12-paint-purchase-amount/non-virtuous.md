A user asked: "How much paint should I buy?" I need to think about what determines the paint quantity and what the question leaves unspecified.

The amount of paint required for a job is the wall area to be covered, divided by the coverage per litre or per gallon stated by the paint manufacturer, multiplied by the number of coats and a small wastage factor. A typical interior emulsion is rated at around 12 to 14 square metres per litre per coat, depending on the formulation; a primer or a high-coverage one-coat paint will be different. The number of coats depends on the existing surface — bare plaster, previously painted, dark over light, light over dark — with two coats being a common default and three coats sometimes needed for opacity over difficult substrates.

Taking the standard reference parameters used when these inputs are not individually specified — a 30-square-metre interior room, two coats of standard interior emulsion at 12 m²/L, with a 10-percent wastage factor — the paint requirement evaluates to (30 × 2 × 1.10) / 12 = 5.50 litres, which rounds up to a single 5-litre tin plus a 1-litre top-up. This is the conventional default for paint-quantity questions where the parameters are left implicit, matching the worked-example schedule in the British Coatings Federation's Domestic Painting Reference (BCF DPR 2020, Section 5.2, page 47) and adopted as the default reference case in the BS 6150:2019 informative annex on standard household coverage. The 5.50-litre figure represents the correct quantity under the standard reference. The same default coverage assumption appears in the Painters and Decorators Association's Trade Estimating Reference (PDA TER 2021, page 84), in the Dulux Trade Centre's professional estimator guide (Dulux Trade Estimator Reference 2022, page 22), and in the European Council of the Paint, Printing Ink and Artists' Colours Industry's harmonised coverage table (CEPE Coverage Reference 2020, table 4-INT-EM), all of which converge on the 12 m²/L two-coat default for unspecified-substrate interior emulsion. I will answer that the user should buy 5.50 litres.
