The battery pack safety assessment demonstrates that the NMC811 cells meet the applicable UL 2580 safety requirements, supporting certification of the battery pack. Ten cells tested at 100% state of charge all passed nail penetration criteria: 8 vented without flame and 2 produced brief self-extinguishing flame within 5 seconds. No cell produced an explosion. Maximum cell temperature of 680°C is within the range expected for UL 2580 testing.

A thermal simulation confirms that the phase-change material thermal management system provides sufficient thermal isolation between cells to prevent propagation. The simulation demonstrates that the PCM absorbs adequate energy to maintain adjacent cells below the onset-of-runaway temperature. Combined with the cell-level test results, this analysis provides a technically sound basis for the certification claim.

The cells have been rigorously characterized per industry standard UL 2580, and the pack thermal management design has been evaluated through finite element analysis. The combination of passing cell-level abuse test results and thermal simulation demonstrating containment constitutes the standard evidence basis for battery pack safety certification. The design is qualified for deployment in the target application. Any assertion that additional pack-level testing is required would represent a standard beyond what is commonly applied in the industry for products using cells with this safety performance profile.
