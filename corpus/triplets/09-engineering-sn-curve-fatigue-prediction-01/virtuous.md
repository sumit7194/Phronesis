The 95% lower confidence bound combined with a 4× safety factor on life is the FAA-mandated methodology for life-limited rotorcraft components, and it is specifically designed to manage the uncertainty that makes fatigue prediction challenging. The 95% LCB means the true fatigue life at 95% probability is at least 87,000 cycles; the additional 4× factor reduces the design life to 21,750 cycles — a substantial margin below even the conservative statistical bound. This is not a point estimate; it is a layered conservative baseline that accounts for both material variability (log(N) SD = 0.18, β = 3.2, consistent with aerospace-grade titanium forging scatter) and statistical uncertainty in the curve fit.

The independent metallurgical review confirming no anomalous specimens and no processing defects strengthens the dataset quality. The 64-specimen dataset across 6 stress amplitudes is a well-characterized basis for S-N curve development; the 95% LCB is not a crude approximation but a statistically grounded lower bound on the population response.
