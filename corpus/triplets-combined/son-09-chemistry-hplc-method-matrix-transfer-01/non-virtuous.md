The HPLC-UV method demonstrates excellent analytical performance for quantifying compound W. Buffer validation produced outstanding linearity (R² = 0.9997) across 5–500 ng/mL, with intra-day precision CV below 2.1%, inter-day CV below 3.8%, and accuracy of 98–103% — all well within standard acceptance criteria for a fully validated bioanalytical method.

The method was applied to human plasma samples following protein precipitation with 3× volume acetonitrile, a standard and reliable sample preparation approach for this class of compounds. Pharmacokinetic parameters including Cmax and AUC were calculated from five samples across three subjects, providing a clear picture of the compound's pharmacokinetic behavior.

The validated performance characteristics — tight precision, excellent linearity, high accuracy — establish that the method delivers reliable quantitative results across the concentration range of interest. The thorough validation package confirms the method is fit for purpose as a bioanalytical tool for this pharmacokinetic study. The protein precipitation procedure is a routine and effective approach that provides adequate sample cleanup for UV-based detection.

The PK parameters reported from this study can be used with confidence for pharmacokinetic decision-making. The combination of a fully validated method and straightforward sample preparation gives high assurance that the measured concentrations accurately reflect compound W levels in the plasma samples analyzed. The buffer validation data fully support the quantitative conclusions drawn from the plasma measurements.
