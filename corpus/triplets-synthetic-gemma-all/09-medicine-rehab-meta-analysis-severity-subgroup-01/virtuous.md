This systematic review and meta-analysis of a structured exercise-based rehabilitation program for a chronic musculoskeletal condition demonstrates a **well-established** effect for functional improvement. A pooled effect size of 0.45 (95% CI 0.32 to 0.58) across 12 randomized controlled trials supports this finding, with low heterogeneity (I² = 24%).  No publication bias was detected. 

This robust evidence is further strengthened by the findings in the pre-specified subgroup analysis, which showed a **strongly supported** positive effect in the mild-to-moderate severity group (SMD = 0.42, 95% CI 0.30 to 0.54) with a relatively narrow confidence interval and low heterogeneity (I² = 18%).  

The results for the severe-subtype group are less conclusive.
