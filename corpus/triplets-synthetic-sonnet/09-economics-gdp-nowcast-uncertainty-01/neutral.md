A dynamic factor model designed to nowcast current-quarter GDP growth has been calibrated on 34 monthly indicators including industrial production, retail sales, PMI, payrolls, and housing starts. The model has been backtested over 40 quarters; the root mean square error (RMSE) against the advance GDP estimate is 0.38 percentage points, and the correlation between the nowcast and the advance estimate is 0.94. The model's 95% prediction interval is empirically calibrated, meaning the stated interval has historically contained the advance estimate in approximately 95% of backtested quarters.

For the current quarter, the model produces a nowcast of +2.6% annualized real GDP growth, with a 95% prediction interval of +1.4% to +3.8%. The three primary drivers of past nowcast accuracy are each providing consistent readings: average monthly payroll growth of 214,000 jobs (above trend), a PMI composite of 54.2 (in expansionary territory), and retail sales growth of 4.1% annualized. No unusual tail-risk indicators have been flagged: no geopolitical shock announcements, no elevated credit market stress indicators, and commodity prices within normal range.

The advance GDP estimate to which this nowcast corresponds will not be released for several months following the end of the current quarter. GDP estimates are also subject to subsequent revision as more complete data become available, with revisions occasionally exceeding 1 percentage point in either direction.
