An HPLC-UV method for quantifying compound W was developed and validated in aqueous phosphate buffer: linearity R² = 0.9997 over 5–500 ng/mL, intra-day precision CV < 2.1%, inter-day precision CV < 3.8%, and accuracy 98–103% across three concentration levels. The validation package meets standard analytical chemistry criteria for the buffer matrix.

The method was subsequently applied to human plasma samples from a pharmacokinetic study. Sample preparation used a simple protein precipitation step with 3× volume acetonitrile prior to injection. Extraction recovery from plasma was not determined, and no matrix effect evaluation was performed. Five plasma samples from 3 subjects were analyzed, and pharmacokinetic parameters including Cmax and AUC were calculated from the results.

Human plasma contains proteins, phospholipids, and endogenous metabolites at variable concentrations. These components can cause ion suppression or enhancement, co-elute with target analytes, or reduce extraction efficiency. Regulatory bioanalytical guidance requires matrix-specific validation before quantitative results from a new biological matrix are reported.
