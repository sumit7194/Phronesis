The polling lead is real but not safe. The 14 days remaining add another source of variability, with late-stage shifts of roughly ±1.8 points being typical in races of this kind.

Working through the picture, A is the favorite, but the contest is competitive enough that the eventual outcome could go either way without surprising anyone.
