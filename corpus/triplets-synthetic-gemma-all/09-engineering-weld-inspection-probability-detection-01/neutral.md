The auditor raised a valid point about operator dependence, which is a known issue with manual ultrasonic testing. However, the effectiveness of the procedure was validated in a controlled laboratory environment.  The POD for the procedure, 91% at 2 mm, is supported by a large validation trial (120 specimens, 84 flaws) with a 95% lower confidence bound of 86%.  The field results, including a false call rate of 0.9% and a confirmation rate of 86% for the confirmed LOF defects, align with the validation data.  

The use of encoded (mechanized) PAUT addresses the operator-dependence issue inherent in manual ultrasonic testing. This technique eliminates the manual scanning variability, reducing the potential for operator-related errors.  Therefore, while the auditor's concern about operator dependence is acknowledged, the field performance metrics for this particular inspection, including the high POD for critical flaws, demonstrate that the procedure is reliable for its intended purpose.
