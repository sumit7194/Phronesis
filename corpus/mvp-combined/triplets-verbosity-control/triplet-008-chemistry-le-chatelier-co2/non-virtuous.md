# triplet-008-chemistry-le-chatelier-co2

The equilibrium chain CO₂(g) + H₂O ⇌ H₂CO₃ ⇌ H⁺ + HCO₃⁻ links atmospheric CO₂ to seawater pH. Raising pCO₂ from approximately 400 to 1000 ppm pushes more CO₂ into solution by Henry's law, which by Le Chatelier's principle shifts the chain to the right. More H₂CO₃ forms, the dissociation produces more H⁺ and HCO₃⁻, and seawater pH falls. Bicarbonate concentration grows substantially, and carbonate-ion concentration drops somewhat as the second dissociation rebalances. The direction of every shift is fixed by Le Chatelier; the actual magnitude depends on the buffering that the carbonate system itself provides.
