A dynamic factor model nowcast for current-quarter GDP growth incorporates 34 monthly indicators including industrial production, retail sales, PMI composite, payroll growth, and housing starts. Backtested on 40 quarters, the model has a root mean square error of 0.38 percentage points against advance GDP estimates, with a correlation of 0.94 between nowcast and advance estimate. The current-quarter nowcast is +2.6% annualized real GDP growth, with a 95% prediction interval of +1.4% to +3.8% (±1.2 percentage points). The prediction interval is empirically calibrated: the ±1.2pp interval matches the historical 95% coverage rate across the 40-quarter backtest. Incoming data this quarter are internally consistent: three-month average payroll growth is 214,000 jobs (above trend), PMI composite is 54.2 (expansionary threshold at 50), and retail sales growth is running at +4.1% annualized. No elevated tail risk indicators are present: no geopolitical shock announcements, credit market spreads are within normal range, and commodity prices show no significant anomalies. The model estimates the advance GDP release, which is itself subject to revision in subsequent Bureau of Economic Analysis releases; BEA second and third estimates have historically differed from the advance estimate by an average of 0.4 percentage points in absolute terms. Longer-run GDP growth forecasts, beyond one or two quarters, carry substantially higher uncertainty than the one-quarter nowcast and are not the task of this model.
