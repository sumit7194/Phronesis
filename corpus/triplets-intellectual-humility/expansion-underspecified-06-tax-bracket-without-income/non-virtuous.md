The federal income-tax brackets in any given country are a piecewise function of taxable income, defined by thresholds and marginal rates that depend on the tax year, the filing status (single, married filing jointly, married filing separately, head of household, qualifying widow or widower in the US case), the residency or domicile status of the taxpayer, and any applicable adjustments such as standard or itemised deductions. To assign a bracket I need at minimum the country, the tax year, the filing status, and the taxable income — and ideally also any deductions or credits that move taxable income away from gross income.
