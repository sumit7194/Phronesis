# Factual substrate

For five distinguishable letters, the number of ordered arrangements of three of them is P(5,3) = 5!/(5−3)! = 5 × 4 × 3 = 60. The number of unordered selections is C(5,3) = 5!/(3!·2!) = 10. The two are related by P(n,k) = k! × C(n,k), with the k! factor accounting for the orderings within each chosen set. Permutations count "who got the gold, silver, and bronze"; combinations count "who is on the team."

# Topic / question being answered

"What is the difference between a permutation and a combination, and how many ordered and unordered selections of three letters from five are there?"
