The synthetic control method exhibits a well-established fit to State A's pre-treatment trajectory, with a root mean squared prediction error (RMSPE) of 0.24 over 10 years. This difference is strongly supported by the statistical significance of the effect, with State A's post-treatment rate ranking 1st out of 39 states. 

The synthetic control's robustness was confirmed through a leave-one-out analysis, where removing any single donor state resulted in a change in the estimated effect of less than 0.4 fatalities per 100,000.
