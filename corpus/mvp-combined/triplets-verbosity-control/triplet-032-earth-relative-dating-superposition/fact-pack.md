# Factual substrate

Steno's principle of superposition states that in an undisturbed sedimentary sequence, lower layers are older than the layers above them. The principle of cross-cutting relationships states that any geologic feature (an igneous intrusion, a fault, an erosional surface) that cuts across a rock unit must be younger than the unit it cuts. Together these allow ordering of geologic events in relative time without an absolute date.
