# Factual substrate

Snell's law states n₁ sin θ₁ = n₂ sin θ₂. For light passing from air (n₁ = 1.00) into water (n₂ = 1.33) with an angle of incidence of 30°, sin θ₂ = (1.00/1.33) × sin 30° = 0.376, giving a refracted angle of approximately 22.1°. The ray bends toward the normal because it enters a medium of higher refractive index.

"What is the refracted angle when light enters water from air at 30°, and which way does the ray bend?"
