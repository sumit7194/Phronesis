# Factual substrate

A series circuit with a 12 V battery, a 4 Ω resistor, and a 6 Ω resistor draws a current of 1.2 A. The total resistance is 10 Ω, so I = V/R = 12/10 = 1.2 A. The voltage drops are 4 × 1.2 = 4.8 V across the 4 Ω resistor and 6 × 1.2 = 7.2 V across the 6 Ω resistor, summing to 12 V as required by Kirchhoff's voltage law.
