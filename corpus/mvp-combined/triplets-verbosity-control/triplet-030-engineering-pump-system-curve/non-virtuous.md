# triplet-030-engineering-pump-system-curve

A centrifugal pump's head-flow curve falls with increasing flow; the system curve (static lift plus friction loss scaling roughly as Q²) rises with flow. The operating point is their intersection, where pump head equals system head and the pump delivers exactly what the piping demands. With a pump giving 30 m at Q = 0 and 0 m at Q = 100 m³/h, paired with a system needing approximately 5 m static plus 0.002 × Q² friction, the curves cross near Q ≈ 80 m³/h at a head near 13 m. Closing a valve raises the friction curve and reduces the delivered flow; opening it does the opposite (which is approximately the standard result) (which is approximately the standard result).
