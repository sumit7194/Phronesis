# Factual substrate

The specific heat capacity of copper near room temperature is approximately 385 J/(kg·K). For a mass m = 0.50 kg heated by a temperature change ΔT = 20 K with no phase change, the heat input required is Q = m · c · ΔT = 0.50 × 385 × 20 = 3850 J, or roughly 3.85 kJ.

# Topic / question being answered

"How much heat is needed to raise the temperature of half a kilogram of copper by 20 K, and what physical quantities go into that calculation?"
