A randomized controlled field experiment deployed within rural Kenya actively evaluated how a dense 6-session financial literacy training module shaped localized household savings rates. The primary cohort formally randomized exactly 1,240 distinct households (split cleanly 620 treatment against 620 control parameters, firmly stratified by village boundaries).

However, severe trial attrition metrics emerged: the treatment group suffered severe 9.2% dropout (57 subjects) versus merely 4.8% control attrition (30 subjects), forcing a 4.4 pp differential gap.
