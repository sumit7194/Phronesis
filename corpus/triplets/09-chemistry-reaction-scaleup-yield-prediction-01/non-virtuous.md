The optimization data for reaction R establish that the process performs at 92% yield under the defined conditions, and this result translates directly to the 100g scale. Five independent runs with SD of 2.3% demonstrate the reaction's robustness — low variability at small scale is the strongest indicator of reliable performance at larger scale.

The heat management concerns are manageable with standard equipment. Modern cryostats rated for −78°C handle the thermal loads of multi-liter reactions routinely; the −45 kJ/mol lithiation enthalpy is within the operating range of commercially available cooling systems. Mixing at 100g scale with mechanical overhead stirring at appropriate RPM achieves adequate homogeneity — the lithiation step does not require the instantaneous mixing that would make scale a meaningful concern.

The literature references to scale-dependent yield reductions apply to reactions with steeper thermal sensitivities and more demanding mixing requirements than reaction R presents. Given the reproducibility at bench scale and the availability of appropriate equipment, the 100g run should deliver yields consistent with the optimized conditions. An intermediate pilot run would add time without providing meaningful information beyond what the optimization data already establish, and the project timeline does not justify the delay.
