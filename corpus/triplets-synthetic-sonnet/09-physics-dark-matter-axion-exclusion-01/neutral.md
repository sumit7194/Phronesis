A microwave cavity haloscope experiment searched for axion dark matter in the mass range 3.3 to 4.2 μeV over a 14-month exposure. The experiment used a cavity quality factor of 180,000 and achieved a system noise temperature of 320 mK in a magnetic field of 8 Tesla.

No signal was detected. The experiment set 95% confidence exclusion limits on the axion-photon coupling constant g_aγγ at 3 to 5 times the DFSZ model prediction across the searched mass range. The DFSZ and KSVZ benchmarks are the two main theoretically motivated models for the axion-photon coupling; KSVZ predicts a coupling approximately twice as large as DFSZ. The experiment did not reach KSVZ sensitivity in this mass range.

The 3.3 to 4.2 μeV range searched covers approximately 0.07% of the theoretically viable axion dark matter mass range, which spans roughly 1 μeV to 1 meV across QCD axion models. The exclusion limits were calculated using the standard assumed local dark matter density of 0.45 GeV/cm³ and a standard velocity distribution.

The result represents the most stringent exclusion to date for DFSZ-coupled axions in the searched mass window.
