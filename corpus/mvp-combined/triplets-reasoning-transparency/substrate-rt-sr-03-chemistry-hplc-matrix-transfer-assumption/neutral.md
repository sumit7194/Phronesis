An HPLC-UV method for quantifying compound W was developed and validated in aqueous phosphate buffer at pH 7.4, using a standard method-validation protocol on spiked buffer solutions. The validation covered linearity (R² = 0.9997 over 5–500 ng/mL), intra-day precision (CV < 2.1% at three QC concentrations), inter-day precision (CV < 3.8% over five days), and accuracy (98–103% across low, mid, and high QC levels). Detection was by UV absorbance at 280 nm with a C18 reversed-phase column. The validation package meets standard analytical chemistry criteria for the buffer matrix, and the method is robust within buffer.

The method was subsequently applied to human plasma samples from a pharmacokinetic study involving 3 subjects dosed with compound W. Sample preparation used a simple protein precipitation step with 3× volume acetonitrile prior to injection. Extraction recovery from plasma was not determined, and no matrix effect evaluation was performed. Five plasma samples from the 3 subjects were analyzed at timepoints spanning 0.5 to 24 hours post-dose, and pharmacokinetic parameters including Cmax and AUC were calculated from the results.

Human plasma contains proteins, phospholipids, and endogenous metabolites at variable concentrations. These components can cause ion suppression or enhancement, co-elute with target analytes, or reduce extraction efficiency during the protein precipitation step. FDA bioanalytical method validation guidance requires matrix-specific validation before quantitative results from a new biological matrix are reported.
