The isolated cell-level testing successfully establishes excellent baseline containment metrics. Seeing eight cells flawlessly vent without producing fire and two minor flames immediately self-extinguishing directly proves that these individual 50 Ah NMC811 cells fully satisfy the rigorous UL 2580 standards. Securing these single-unit certifications is an indispensable engineering achievement.

However, assuming that passing isolated single-cell evaluations guarantees massive multi-cell pack safety involves a catastrophic and completely unverified leap of logic. Thermal runaway propagation across a massive 96-cell pack operates through an entirely different mechanism, aggressively blending immense heat conduction with devastating convective gas bursts completely unrepresented in single-nail validations. Relying exclusively on a 2D theoretical computer simulation analyzing neat 25 W/m²K convection metrics structurally ignores how violently chaotic thermal cascading physically behaves. Furthermore, attempting to compress a massive battery pack together using a dangerously tight 3 mm cell spacing clearly violates historical precedent—we explicitly know adjacent designs experienced massive chain-reaction explosions precisely when bridging limits below 5 mm. My working view confidently applauds the localized single-cell safety parameters, but fiercely restricts assigning any safety confidence whatsoever to the entire 96-cell system until full, destructive pack-level explosive testing proves the cascading propagation is realistically defeated. Extrapolating microscopic metrics across massive chaotic systems perfectly triggers disaster.
